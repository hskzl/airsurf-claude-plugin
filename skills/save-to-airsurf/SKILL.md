# Save the outcome to Airsurf

1. Decide the right shape and say it in one line before writing:
   - decisions and notes from the conversation: `save_chat` (a page with a title, summary, decisions and links);
   - follow-up work: `create_issues` in the right team (`workspace_info` lists teams);
   - progress on the issue at hand: `comment` on it.
2. Keep it short and factual. Link the related issue, page or pull request.
3. Return the links. Every change can be undone with `undo_change`.
